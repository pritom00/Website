About me

I am an accounting student in Dhaka who enjoys it when the numbers reconcile. I use Excel to build and check models, Power BI to show what the data says, and Figma, Word and PowerPoint to make reports that a busy manager can read in a few minutes.

My goal is to become a Chartered Accountant (CA). I speak English comfortably, which helps me explain figures to clients and teammates, and I want to start in audit or tax.
